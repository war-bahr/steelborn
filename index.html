SETTINGS

Auto picks a level for your phone and lowers resolution, then bloom, if combat drops below ~42 FPS. Changing quality reloads the game.

HOW TO PLAY

HANGAR

Swap Head, Core, each Arm, Legs, Back and a weapon in each hand. Every part changes armor, weight, speed, energy, damage and reach. Drag the mech to spin it, pinch to zoom. Paint it, pick a finish, wear and a decal. You have 3 build slots, saved automatically.

MOVE & FIGHT

Drag on the left half to move. LIGHT chains a combo that alternates arms. HEAVY is your weapon's big move; after 2+ lights it becomes a stronger Ender. Two different weapon types unlock a 4th-hit Mixed Combo.

DEFEND

DASH makes you untouchable for a moment (costs energy). Hold GUARD to block. Tap GUARD right before a hit lands to PARRY: it staggers the attacker and bats drone shots back. Red circles on the ground mean a slam is coming.

OVERDRIVE

Attacking heats your core. More heat means more damage, and the red zone is Overdrive. Hit 100% and you overheat and stall to vent. Cores and coolers change how fast you heat and cool.

FINISHER

Hits, kills and parries fill the ring on FINISH. When it glows, tap for a slow-motion execution on the nearest enemy.

PARTS

Enemies drop part crates. Walk over them, or they fly to you when the wave ends. The Warden boss drops its own signature parts.
